Terms and Conditions

Online Portal · Version 1 · Last updated November 23, 2026

These terms explain how you may use this portal. By ticking the box on the sign-in or registration screen, you confirm that you have read and agree to them.

1. Who may use the portal

The portal is for lecturers and for students enrolled in a lecturer's class. Use your own account only. Keep your password private, and do not let anyone else sign in as you. You are responsible for what is done with your account.

2. Acceptable use

  • Use the portal only for your classes: attendance, activities, exams, scores and class communication.
  • Do not try to access another person's account or records, interfere with the portal, or share exam content, answers or class materials without your lecturer's permission.
  • Give accurate information when you register or update your details.

3. Online exams and academic integrity

Online exams are taken under your school's rules on academic honesty. Cheating, getting help that is not allowed, or letting someone else take an exam for you may lead to disciplinary action by your lecturer or school.

To keep exams fair, the portal may apply exam rules while you are taking an exam. These can include limits on leaving the exam screen, signing in on another device, and the time allowed. If you break these rules, your exam may be submitted automatically with the answers saved so far. Your lecturer decides whether you may continue or retake the exam.

4. Use of the camera during examinations

During an online exam, the portal uses your device's camera to take photographs for exam integrity. By agreeing to these terms and allowing camera access, you consent to this.

  • Photographs are taken only while an exam is in progress. How often and when they are taken may change depending on the exam and the circumstances.
  • The photographs are used only to check that the person taking the exam is the enrolled student and that the exam was taken fairly.
  • They are stored privately and are visible only to the lecturer of the class and the portal administrators who maintain the system. They are not shown to other students.
  • They are kept only as long as needed to review the exam and any related concern, and then may be deleted.
  • Your browser asks you to allow the camera. If you do not allow it, you will not be able to start an exam that requires it. Talk to your lecturer if you cannot use a camera.

5. Your information

The portal keeps the information needed to run your classes, such as your name, student number, contact details, program, attendance, scores, exam answers and profile photo. It is used only for teaching and class administration, and it is visible only to the people who need it for that purpose, such as your lecturers. We do not sell your information.

6. Availability

We work to keep the portal available, but it may be unavailable at times for maintenance or because of problems with your connection or device. Keep your device charged and your connection stable during exams.

7. Suspending access

Access may be suspended or removed if these terms are broken, if an account is shared, or if you are no longer enrolled.

8. Changes to these terms

These terms may be updated. When they change in an important way, you will be asked to agree again.

9. Questions

Ask your lecturer if you have a question about these terms or about how your information is used.
